What NicNacs Is

Affordable Goods

A thrift store stocked by our neighbors, for our neighbors — clothing, household goods, and furniture priced so every family in Grants Pass can find what it needs.

Community Support

Through the Roundelay Project, our community resource exchange, neighbors share goods and mutual aid — and shoppers can pay a dollar or two forward for the next person in line.

Sustainable Recycling

Keeping usable goods in circulation is recycling at its most practical. Dedicated in-store recycling services are a future phase of NicNacs as we grow.

Our Mission

Helping people help people: NicNacs provides affordable goods, community support, and sustainable recycling solutions that uplift individuals and strengthen the Grants Pass community — powered by earned revenue from thrift retail and recycled materials, so the mission sustains itself.

Read our story

Born From Survival. Built For Support.

NicNacs was co-founded together by Frank Shields, Nic Long and Mike Torres. Nic is a three-time liver transplant survivor; during recovery from his third transplant, his father was hospitalized and did not survive — a loss in the middle of his own fight to live. That is why no family should face crisis alone.
