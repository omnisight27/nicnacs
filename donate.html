Donate

Every donation to NicNacs becomes affordable inventory for a Grants Pass neighbor — and helps keep usable goods out of the landfill. Thank you for thinking of us.

What to Donate

  • Clothing — clean, gently used clothing and shoes for all ages
  • Household goods — kitchenware, small appliances in working order, linens, décor, books, and toys
  • Furniture — in good, usable condition

When in doubt, ask — email us at nicnacsthriftstore@gmail.com and we’ll let you know if we can take it.

How to Drop Off

Donations will be accepted at the store: 126 NE Steiger Street, Grants Pass, OR 97526. We’re preparing to open now, and drop-off details are coming soon — watch this page and we’ll post them as soon as the doors are ready.

The Roundelay Project

The Roundelay Project is NicNacs’ community resource exchange — neighbors sharing household goods, mutual aid, and everyday assistance. It is a self-reinforcing loop: donated goods become affordable inventory, sales sustain the store, and every exchange brings the community through our doors as shoppers, donors, and volunteers.

Part of Roundelay is our pay-it-forward board: at the register, any shopper can cover $1–$5 toward the next person’s purchase. A small kindness, posted on the board, waiting for the neighbor who needs it. That’s what we mean by helping people, help people.
