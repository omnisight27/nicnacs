Our Story

Born from survival. Built for support. NicNacs was co-founded together by Frank Shields, Nic Long and Mike Torres.

Nic Long is a three-time liver transplant survivor. During recovery from his third transplant, his father was hospitalized and did not survive — a loss in the middle of Nic’s own fight to live. That is why no family should face crisis alone, and why no usable good should go to waste while a neighbor needs it. Every part of NicNacs — the thrift store, the Roundelay exchange, the recycling hub — exists to turn that promise into something a family can walk through the door and use.

It starts with one person, one family, one neighbor facing a setback — and finding a door that’s open.

Our Mission

Helping people help people: NicNacs provides affordable goods, community support, and sustainable recycling solutions that uplift individuals and strengthen the Grants Pass community — powered by earned revenue from thrift retail and recycled materials, so the mission sustains itself.

Our Vision

A thriving circular economy where nothing goes to waste and no family faces crisis alone — a community exchange strong enough to pay for itself while lifting everyone it touches.

NicNacs isn’t asking for charity — it’s bringing Grants Pass a model that works: community-powered retail that funds its own mission. We’re ready to open our doors at 126 NE Steiger St and get to work.
